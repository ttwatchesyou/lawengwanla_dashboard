The runtime crossfades the two recorded spool textures with inertia and
pitch/airflow filtering according to robot speed and distance to the next stop.
A separately enveloped recorded valve release follows a sustained boost drop.
These are edited real recordings, not an exact reproduction of every turbo.

demo.wav: 10-second offline render of this runtime using the processed samples
above, two spool-up / lift-off cycles at 75% preview volume. Same source credits
and CC BY 3.0 attribution apply to the edited HKS recording in this preview.
