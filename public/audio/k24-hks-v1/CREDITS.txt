v3 demo.wav: 12-second offline render of the exact runtime, including a natural
end, complete pressure release, quiet engine settling and silence afterward.
All licensed-source credits above apply. This is an interpretation of the
reference, not a guarantee of 100% perceptual identity.

Corner engine refinement: engine pitch and level ease down over the last 0.8 m
before a span endpoint, even when measured wheel speed is steady. A gentle
7–12 Hz amplitude variation adds a low-rev cadence. The next span smoothly
raises RPM again; straight-through cells retain their full-span distance.
corner-demo.wav uses these same licensed sources in a 12-second runtime render,
with a corner at 3 s, final stop at 6.2 s and a complete release/engine tail.

Valve pressure refinement: vent once on approach within 0.5 m when boost starts
falling. The recorded release is approximately 4 dB louder, with slower playback
and a soft, filtered broadband air layer fading over 0.85–1.2 seconds. That air
layer is generated by the runtime; all recorded-source credits above still apply.
A fresh rise in boost is required before another vent can trigger.
pressure-demo.wav: 12-second runtime render with two approaches and full tails.

Corner lift-off pause: a 0.46-second valve/air burst is followed by a 20 ms
fade-out, 180 ms silence and 140 ms fade-in. The gate covers both the engine
and turbo, and is scheduled in the audio clock so UI polling cannot erase it.
Releases created only at final completion keep their original full tail.
pause-demo.wav: 12-second runtime render using the same credited recordings.

Latest corner refinement (supersedes the corner pause above): no silence gate.
Each approach triggers two valve/air pulses 280 ms apart. The first pulse lasts
360 ms and the second 460 ms, with lower pressure on the second. The engine and
turbo bed continue under both pulses. Final-only releases retain their full tail.
double-valve-demo.wav: 12-second runtime render, same credited recordings.

Lower second-pulse pressure: both the second recorded valve burst and its air
layer use 50% gain, in addition to the existing lower boost parameter. The first
pulse and 280 ms spacing are unchanged. lower-pressure-demo.wav is a runtime
render using the same credited source recordings.

Latest flutter: four pulses spaced 100 ms apart, with decreasing pressure and
relative gain 1.0 / 0.64 / 0.40 / 0.22. Each pulse lasts 120–140 ms and starts
90 ms into the licensed valve sample to retain its air transient. The engine
and turbo remain continuous. Final-only releases still use the full recording.

CURRENT ENGINE PROFILE: K24 Turbo-inspired synthesis + recorded HKS valve.
engine-low.wav / engine-high.wav are original procedural engine sounds built by
scripts/build-k24-engine.py: four-cylinder/four-stroke combustion pulse train,
unequal cylinder strengths, exhaust resonances and filtered intake noise.
Reference: "RAW SOUND - K24 TURBO" by IMAHKAN
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vdBzOoUfoN0
The reference was analyzed for cadence and broad spectral balance; no waveform
from this video is included. These files are a synthesized interpretation of
K24 Turbo, not a recording of a verified K24. The previous Peugeot engine bed
is no longer used. Low/high load layers crossfade with speed and approach.
